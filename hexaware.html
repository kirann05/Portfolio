Project Context

At Cognizant in Hyderabad, I worked within the IT Services and Digital Transformation domain, delivering enterprise-grade solutions for global clients. The platform enabled operations teams at airports and public venues to monitor Wi-Fi infrastructure in real time, track network performance and user traffic, and automate backup and alerting workflows — all deployed on AWS with containerized microservices for high availability and fault tolerance.
